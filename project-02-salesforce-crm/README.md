# Project 2: Salesforce CRM & Sales Pipeline Analysis

**Status:** Planned  
**Data:** Synthetic B2B CRM records generated for this portfolio  
**Tools:** Salesforce Developer Edition, Salesforce Reports, Tableau Public

## Business problem

A B2B sales manager wants to understand lead conversion, opportunity-stage movement, open pipeline and forecasted value.

## Data source

All CRM records will be synthetic. They will not represent a real company, employee, prospect, customer or commercial result.

## Planned analysis

- Lead volume and conversion by source.
- Opportunity volume and value by stage.
- Closed-won versus closed-lost performance.
- Weighted open pipeline.
- Sales cycle and owner-level performance.

Not yet available. Findings will be based on the generated dataset and clearly labelled as illustrative.
