# Project 1: E-commerce Business Performance & Customer Experience

**Status:** In progress  
**Dataset:** Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset  
**Tools:** PostgreSQL, SQL, Power BI, DAX

## Business problem

An online marketplace operations team needs to understand product GMV, order trends, repeat purchasing, delivery performance and review outcomes to prioritise operational improvements.

## Data source

The Olist dataset contains anonymised marketplace records from 2016–2018. Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce. Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Raw files are not included in this repository; follow the setup instructions added during the data-preparation phase.

## Planned analysis

- Product GMV and delivered-order trends.
- Product category and seller performance.
- Repeat customer rate.
- Delivery duration and late delivery rate.
- Relationship between delivery performance and review scores.
